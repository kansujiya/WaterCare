How we clean your tank

The same seven steps for every rooftop and underground tank, usually 60 to 90 minutes each.

  1. Inspect

    We check the tank and take before photos.

  2. Drain

    Leftover water is drained, or pumped to your other tank if you want to keep it.

  3. Suction

    A sludge pump pulls out silt, mud and settled dirt.

  4. Jet wash

    High-pressure water strips algae, moss and scale off walls, floor and lid.

  5. Disinfect

    A food-safe disinfectant rinse, then a clean water flush.

  6. Dry

    Rinse water is sucked out and the tank is left clean and dry.

  7. Report

    After photos on WhatsApp, then you pay by UPI or cash.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I clean my water tank?

Every 6 months. Gurugram water carries a lot of silt, so tanks build up sludge and algae quickly, especially in summer and the monsoon.

Does anyone go inside the tank?

No. We clean from outside using a sludge suction pump, a high-pressure jet washer and long lances. Nobody steps into your tank.

How long does tank cleaning take?

About 60 to 90 minutes per tank, depending on size and how dirty it is.

Will my water supply be off?

Only for the tank being cleaned, for the duration of the job. If you have two tanks we can clean one at a time.

Which chemicals do you use?

A food-safe disinfectant suited to drinking water tanks, followed by a clean water flush, so no residue is left behind.

How do I pay?

After the job, by UPI or cash. We confirm the final amount on WhatsApp before we arrive.
