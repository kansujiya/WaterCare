Why it matters

Why clean your tank every 6 months?

Silt settles as sludge. Gurugram supply water carries fine silt that builds up as a layer of mud at the bottom of every tank.

Algae loves heat. Rooftop tanks bake in the summer sun, and green growth spreads on the walls and lid.

It's the water you live on. You bathe, cook and wash dishes with it, and often drink it after RO. Stagnant sediment is a place for germs to grow.

The process

How our overhead and underground tank cleaning works

The same seven steps for every tank, done by a trained crew with their own pumps, jet washer and hoses.

  1. Step 01

    Inspect

    We check the tank and take before photos.

  2. Step 02

    Drain

    Leftover water is drained, or moved to your other tank if you want to keep it.

  3. Step 03

    Suction

    A sludge pump pulls out silt, mud and settled dirt.

  4. Step 04

    Jet wash

    High-pressure water strips algae, moss and scale off walls, floor and lid.

  5. Step 05

    Disinfect

    A food-safe disinfectant rinse, then a clean water flush.

  6. Step 06

    Dry

    Rinse water is sucked out and the tank is left clean and dry.

  7. Step 07

    Report

    After photos on WhatsApp, then you pay by UPI or cash.

How often should I clean my water tank?

Every 6 months is the common rule. Gurugram supply water carries silt, so sludge and algae build up quickly, especially after summer and the monsoon.

Does anyone go inside the tank?

No. We clean from outside using a sludge suction pump, a high-pressure jet washer and long lances. Nobody steps into your tank.

How long does tank cleaning take?

About 60 to 90 minutes per tank, depending on size and how dirty it is.

Will my water supply be off?

Only for the tank being cleaned, for the duration of the job. If you have two tanks we clean one at a time.

Which chemicals do you use?

A food-safe disinfectant meant for drinking water tanks, used at the maker's dose and followed by a clean water flush, so no residue is left behind.

How do I pay?

After the job, by UPI or cash. We confirm a fixed quote on WhatsApp before we come, and that is what you pay. No surprise charges after the job.

I don't know my tank size. What should I pick?

Plastic tanks have the liters printed on the side. Most 2 to 3 BHK homes have a 1,000 to 1,500 liter rooftop tank. Pick your best guess and we confirm before starting.
